PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy matters.

This page explains the principal information PostKina may process when you read the site, sign in as a writer, submit content, rate a story or send a suggestion.

Information we may process

  • Account details supplied through Google sign-in, such as name, email address and account identifier.
  • Stories, suggestions, ratings and related timestamps that you choose to submit.
  • Basic device, country, referral and usage information provided by analytics and advertising services, subject to consent choices and their own policies.
  • Locally saved preferences, including saved stories or series.

Why we use it

We use information to operate accounts, publish content, prevent abuse, understand site performance, improve the reader experience and meet legal obligations.

Services

PostKina uses third-party infrastructure and services that may include Firebase, Cloudflare, GitHub Pages, Google Analytics, advertising providers and social-sharing platforms. Opening an external sharing link is subject to that platform's privacy practices.

Your choices

You may avoid signing in, clear browser storage, use available consent controls, or request access, correction or deletion of personal information by contacting lankaknot@gmail.com. Some records may be retained where required for security, legal compliance or legitimate publishing records.

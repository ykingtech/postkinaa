ABOUT POSTKINA

Stories worth understanding.

PostKina is an independent English-language digital publication for readers interested in Europe and the wider world. We turn developing stories into clear, accessible explainers with context, attribution and links to the original reporting used as background.

Our purpose

We want readers to understand what happened, why it matters, how it affects Europe, what came before and what may happen next. We aim to make complex stories easier to follow without presenting speculation as fact.

Who publishes PostKina?

PostKina is operated by the PostKina editorial team. AI tools may assist with research organisation, drafting and formatting, but PostKina remains responsible for what appears on this website.

AI-ASSISTED CONTENT POLICY

AI can assist. Responsibility stays with us.

PostKina uses automated tools to discover public news feeds, organise source material, prepare drafts, suggest headlines and hashtags, locate potentially reusable images and format articles for publication.

What AI must not do

  • Invent facts, quotations, eyewitness accounts or sources.
  • Present speculation as established fact.
  • Closely copy or disguise another publisher's article.
  • Remove required attribution or image-credit information.

Source and structure

AI-assisted news articles should identify the principal source and explain what happened, why it matters, European impact, background, verified sourcing and what may happen next. Automated output can still contain errors; readers should consult the linked source for primary context.

Editorial accountability

Use of AI does not transfer responsibility away from PostKina. We review reported issues and correct substantiated errors. Send concerns to lankaknot@gmail.com.

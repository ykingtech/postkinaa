Daily Europe Briefing: Europe Briefing: Ukraine Power Crisis, Barcelona Strikes, School Threats, and Tennis Shocks

By PostKina Editorial · 12 November 2026 at 08:03 Europe/Paris · 5 min read

A look at the day's top European stories, including Ukraine's critical power situation, protests in Spain, bomb threats in schools across Italy, Scotland, and Northern Ireland, and a significant upset at the WTA Finals.

How we report: This article adds context and explanation to attributed source reporting. AI may assist the editorial workflow; sources, corrections and licensing remain visible to readers.

What happened

Ukraine is facing a critical situation with its power supply following a series of Russian strikes. The attacks have led to widespread power outages, plunging many areas into darkness and cold, significantly impacting daily life, particularly for families.

In Spain, clashes erupted in Barcelona amidst a general strike. Tens of thousands participated in the industrial action, protesting against the rising cost of living and housing issues. The demonstrations saw significant public turnout and led to confrontations.

Schools in Italy, Scotland, and Northern Ireland were targeted by bomb threats. Over 6,000 schools in Italy alone received threatening messages. Authorities in all affected countries have stated that there is no immediate risk to the public, with investigations underway.

An unusual escape occurred in Argentina when a 74-year-old man, Leonardo Bertulazzi, evaded house arrest by abseiling from his balcony. Bertulazzi, a former member of the Italian Marxist guerrilla group the Red Brigades, was awaiting extradition to Italy.

In sports, Linda Noskova delivered a stunning performance at the WTA Finals, defeating Coco Gauff to become the first player to secure a spot in the semi-finals. The victory marked a significant upset in the tournament.

Why it matters

The ongoing Russian attacks on Ukraine's energy infrastructure highlight the severe humanitarian consequences of the conflict, extending beyond direct combat to impact civilian life through lack of heat and light during colder months. The situation underscores the resilience required of the Ukrainian population and the persistent challenges in maintaining essential services under sustained assault. The protests in Spain reflect a broader European concern over economic pressures, with rising inflation and housing costs affecting citizens across the continent. The bomb threats to schools, while deemed not to pose a public risk, disrupt educational activities and create anxiety among students, parents, and staff, raising questions about security measures and the motivations behind such hoaxes. The escape of Leonardo Bertulazzi, a figure with a history of political extremism, raises concerns about his whereabouts and potential activities, especially given his impending extradition. Linda Noskova's decisive victory over Coco Gauff is a major development in women's tennis, signaling a rising talent and potentially reshaping the competitive landscape of top-tier tournaments.

European impact

The energy crisis in Ukraine has ripple effects across Europe, potentially increasing the demand for energy imports and highlighting the continent's interconnected energy security challenges. The cost of living protests in Spain could inspire similar actions in other European nations grappling with inflation and economic inequality. The school bomb threats, while localized to Italy, Scotland, and Northern Ireland, touch upon a wider European concern about security in public institutions and the psychological impact of such threats. The potential implications of Bertulazzi's escape, given his Italian connections, could involve cross-border security cooperation and investigations.

Background and context

Russia's sustained campaign of missile and drone strikes against Ukraine's energy infrastructure began in late 2022 and has continued intermittently. These attacks aim to cripple Ukraine's ability to function and demoralize its population, particularly during winter. The strikes have forced Ukrainian authorities to implement rolling blackouts and emergency power rationing. The general strike in Spain was called by major trade unions, citing concerns over insufficient wage increases to match inflation and a persistent housing affordability crisis. Similar economic anxieties have fueled protests in other European countries. The bomb threats to schools represent a recurring type of security incident that authorities must manage, often involving extensive security responses and investigations into the origins of the threats. Leonardo Bertulazzi's history with the Red Brigades, a far-left militant group active in Italy during the 1970s and 1980s, places him within a context of political violence and international fugitive cases. The WTA Finals is one of the most prestigious events in women's professional tennis, featuring the top eight players of the season.

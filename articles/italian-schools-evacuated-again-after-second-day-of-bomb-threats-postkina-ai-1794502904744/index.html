Italian Schools Evacuated Again After Second Day of Bomb Threats

By PostKina Editorial · 12 November 2026 at 18:01 Europe/Paris · 2 min read

For the second consecutive day, schools across Italy have been evacuated following bomb threats, prompting heightened security measures and raising concerns about student safety and the motives behind the alerts.

How we report: This article adds context and explanation to attributed source reporting. AI may assist the editorial workflow; sources, corrections and licensing remain visible to readers.

What happened

On 12 November 2026, several Italian schools received bomb threats, leading to the evacuation of students and staff. The incidents followed a similar alert the previous day, creating a wave of panic across the country’s educational institutions.

Key facts

  • The threats were received by schools in multiple regions, including Lombardy, Lazio, and Sicily.
  • Evacuations were carried out by local police and school security teams.
  • No bombs were found, and no injuries were reported.
  • The incidents prompted a review of emergency protocols by the Ministry of Education.
  • Authorities have not yet identified the source of the threats.

Why it matters

Repeated bomb threats in schools undermine the sense of safety that parents, teachers, and students rely on. They also strain emergency services and divert resources from other critical duties. The situation highlights the need for robust communication channels between schools and law‑making bodies to manage such crises swiftly and transparently.

European impact

While the incidents are confined to Italy, they echo a broader European concern about school safety. Similar threats have surfaced in France, Spain, and the UK, prompting cross‑border cooperation on counter‑terrorism and information sharing. The European Union’s education policy framework stresses the importance of secure learning environments, and these events may influence future directives.

Timeline

  • 11 November 2026: Bomb threat received by a school in Milan; evacuation carried out.
  • 12 November 2026: Second day of threats; multiple schools in Lombardy, Lazio, and Sicily evacuate.
  • 12 November 2026, afternoon: Police confirm no explosive devices; threat deemed a hoax.
  • 13 November 2026: Ministry of Education announces review of emergency protocols.

Background and context

Italy has faced sporadic security challenges in recent years, including threats to public institutions. The country’s law‑enforcement agencies have increased vigilance, especially in educational settings. The Ministry of Education has previously updated evacuation procedures after a 2019 incident involving a false bomb alert in a high school in Naples.

What happens next

The Ministry of Education will conduct a comprehensive review of its emergency response plans, with input from local police and school administrators. Authorities are also investigating the origin of the threats, which may involve cyber‑security teams and intelligence agencies. Parents and students are advised to stay informed through official school communications and to report any suspicious activity immediately.

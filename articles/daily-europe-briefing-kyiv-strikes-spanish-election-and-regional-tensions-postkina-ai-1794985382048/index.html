Daily Europe Briefing: Kyiv Strikes, Spanish Election, and Regional Tensions

By PostKina Editorial · 18 November 2026 at 08:03 Europe/Paris · 4 min read

Russia targets Kyiv bridges as Spain prepares for a pivotal election. A Russian military plane crashes, and dozens are injured in a Prague train collision. Tensions rise in Asia following alleged Russian airspace incursions, impacting global security.

How we report: This article adds context and explanation to attributed source reporting. AI may assist the editorial workflow; sources, corrections and licensing remain visible to readers.

What happened

Russia has escalated targeting of Kyiv bridges, with a mother describing sheltering during strikes, following Ukraine's prior damage to the Kerch Bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea. Spain prepares for a pivotal general election on November 29, with conservatives and the far-right favored to win, potentially ending eight years of Socialist-led rule. A Russian military plane crashed in Kemerovo, killing all 19 on board, with a technical malfunction preliminarily suspected. In Prague, a low-speed head-on train collision injured dozens. Japan scrambled jets after alleged Russian airspace incursions, with Taiwan and South Korea also responding to Russian military aircraft near their airspaces.

Background and context

The Russia-Ukraine conflict, ongoing since February 2022, strategically targets infrastructure like bridges for military logistics and civilian supply. Ukraine's 2022 Kerch Bridge strike impacted Russia's Crimean routes, leading to Russia's consistent targeting of Ukrainian infrastructure. Spain's fragmented political landscape, with PSOE leading since 2018, now sees rising conservative and far-right support, fueled by immigration, economic stability, and national identity concerns, reflecting a broader European trend. Military aviation incidents occur in large air forces; Russia's history of crashes is often linked to aging equipment or maintenance, monitored by international observers. Railway safety is a constant European concern, with incidents like Prague's prompting protocol reviews.

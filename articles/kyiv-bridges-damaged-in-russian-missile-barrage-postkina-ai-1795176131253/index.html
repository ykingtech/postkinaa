Kyiv Bridges Damaged in Russian Missile Barrage

By PostKina Editorial · 20 November 2026 at 13:02 Europe/Paris · 3 min read

Multiple bridges in Kyiv sustained damage during a significant Russian missile attack, resulting in casualties and disruption. The strikes followed Ukraine's recent operation targeting the Crimean Bridge.

How we report: This article adds context and explanation to attributed source reporting. AI may assist the editorial workflow; sources, corrections and licensing remain visible to readers.

What happened

Kyiv experienced a major Russian missile attack that resulted in damage to at least three bridges within the city. The strikes, which occurred on Friday, November 20, 2026, also led to fatalities and injuries among the civilian population. This wave of attacks follows Ukraine's recent successful targeting of the Crimean Bridge.

Key facts

  • At least three bridges in Kyiv were hit.
  • Five people were killed in the attacks.
  • The strikes were part of a wider wave of Russian missile attacks.
  • The incidents occurred on Friday, November 20, 2026.
  • The attacks followed Ukraine's operation against the Crimean Bridge.

Why it matters

The damage to Kyiv's vital infrastructure, particularly its bridges, has immediate and significant consequences for the city's functionality and the safety of its residents. Such attacks disrupt transportation networks, impede emergency services, and underscore the ongoing nature of the conflict. The targeting of civilian infrastructure also raises concerns about adherence to international humanitarian law. The psychological impact on the population, living under the constant threat of such strikes, is also a critical factor.

European impact

These attacks on Ukraine's capital have broader implications for European security and stability. They highlight the persistent threat posed by the conflict and the potential for escalation. The disruption to Ukraine's internal systems can also affect the flow of humanitarian aid and international support. European nations continue to monitor the situation closely, with many providing substantial assistance to Ukraine and imposing sanctions on Russia. The attacks reinforce the need for continued diplomatic efforts and robust defense cooperation within Europe.

Timeline

Friday, November 20, 2026: A major Russian missile attack targets Kyiv, hitting at least three bridges and causing fatalities. The attacks occur in the wake of Ukraine's operation against the Crimean Bridge.

Background and context

The current situation is a continuation of the broader conflict that began in February 2022. Russia has repeatedly targeted Ukrainian cities and infrastructure with missile and drone attacks. Ukraine, in turn, has conducted operations against Russian military targets and logistical lines, including the recent attack on the Crimean Bridge, a key supply route for Russian forces in occupied southern Ukraine. The Crimean Bridge, officially known as the Kerch Bridge, connects Russia to the Crimean Peninsula, which Russia annexed in 2014. Its strategic importance has made it a frequent target. The attacks on Kyiv's bridges represent a significant escalation in the targeting of civilian infrastructure within the capital.

What happens next

Following the attacks, Ukrainian authorities are expected to assess the full extent of the damage to the bridges and begin repair efforts. Emergency services will continue to provide assistance to those affected. The international community will likely respond with further condemnation of the attacks and potentially increased humanitarian or military aid to Ukraine. Russia's motivations for the strikes will be analyzed, with a focus on whether they represent a retaliatory measure or a broader strategic shift in their military operations. Further missile attacks or defensive measures by Ukraine are also possible as the conflict continues.

Daily Europe Briefing: Ukraine's Underground Front, Cold Case Justice, and High-Profile Allegations

By PostKina Editorial · 19 November 2026 at 08:02 Europe/Paris · 6 min read

Ukrainian forces engage Russian troops in subterranean tunnels in Kharkiv, adapting to drone warfare. Meanwhile, France sees justice in a decades-old child abuse case, and Spanish courts investigate allegations against Julio Iglesias. New footage also sheds light on the Crimea Bridge collapse.

How we report: This article adds context and explanation to attributed source reporting. AI may assist the editorial workflow; sources, corrections and licensing remain visible to readers.

What happened

Ukrainian forces are reportedly engaging Russian troops in an old Soviet gas pipeline in the Kharkiv region, establishing a new front of underground warfare. This subterranean approach allows Ukrainian soldiers to operate beyond Russian drone reach, a critical adaptation to modern battlefield surveillance, as reported by the BBC.

In France, a decades-old cold case concluded with the jailing of parents for the torture and death of their four-year-old daughter, Inass Touloub, in 1987. Her body, found along the A10 motorway with signs of severe abuse, remained unidentified until 2018, bringing a delayed but significant judicial outcome.

Spanish judicial authorities initiated a court investigation into allegations of sexual offences against the 83-year-old singer Julio Iglesias. Two former employees accused him of incidents reportedly occurring in 2021 at his Caribbean residences, placing a prominent European cultural figure under legal scrutiny.

Finally, new video footage analyzed by BBC Verify depicts explosions immediately preceding the collapse of a section of the Crimea Bridge. This analysis provides visual evidence detailing the destruction to the strategically vital link, offering further insight into a key event in the Ukraine conflict.

Why it matters

The shift to underground warfare in Ukraine signifies a crucial adaptation to pervasive drone technology, fundamentally reshaping modern battlefields. By operating below ground, Ukrainian forces aim to neutralize Russia's aerial surveillance advantage, potentially influencing future military tactics globally.

The resolution of the Inass Touloub case after 39 years underscores the enduring pursuit of justice and forensic science's dedication in solving cold cases. This outcome brings accountability for horrific child abuse, sending a clear message that severe crimes against vulnerable individuals will be investigated across decades.

The investigation into Julio Iglesias, a globally recognized cultural icon, carries significant weight due to his public stature. It reflects an ongoing societal reckoning with allegations of misconduct, particularly in the wake of movements advocating for victims. Such high-profile cases often prompt broader discussions about accountability.

New video evidence concerning the Crimea Bridge collapse is important for clarifying circumstances surrounding a pivotal event in the Ukraine conflict. The bridge is a critical logistical and symbolic link for Russia; visual confirmation of explosions preceding its damage provides crucial factual detail, aiding understanding of the conflict's dynamics.

Background and context

The Ukraine conflict has seen dynamic tactical evolution, driven by drone technology. Widespread drone use for reconnaissance and targeting makes open-field operations perilous, forcing adaptation. The reported shift to underground warfare in old infrastructure, like gas pipelines, directly responds to this, aiming to neutralize aerial advantages.

The Inass Touloub case highlights the enduring challenges and triumphs of cold case investigations. Advances in forensic science, particularly DNA analysis, are instrumental in solving previously intractable cases. The long delay in identification and prosecution underscores persistent investigative efforts and the critical need for robust child protection systems.

Julio Iglesias's legal situation arises within a global context of heightened awareness regarding sexual misconduct allegations, often associated with the #MeToo movement. This movement has empowered many to come forward, leading to investigations against prominent figures. The Spanish court's decision reflects a legal system responding to such claims.

The Crimea Bridge, a crucial link between mainland Russia and annexed Crimea, has been a significant target and symbol throughout the conflict. Its construction was a major political project for Russia, and its operational status is vital for military logistics. New evidence, like the analyzed video footage, contributes to understanding this strategic infrastructure's vulnerability.

What happens next

In Ukraine, the development of underground warfare tactics suggests a potential diversification of combat strategies. Observers will monitor if this approach becomes more widespread, influencing the conflict's dynamics, particularly in areas with entrenched front lines. The effectiveness of these tactics against Russian countermeasures will be key.

Regarding the French cold case, with the parents now jailed, legal proceedings have largely concluded. Attention may now shift to broader implications for child protection policies and ongoing efforts to resolve other historical abuse cases across France and Europe.

For Julio Iglesias, the court investigation in Spain marks the beginning of a formal legal process. This will involve further evidence gathering, testimonies, and potentially a trial. The outcome will be closely watched, given the high-profile nature of the accused.

Concerning the Crimea Bridge, the new video analysis provides further factual basis for understanding the incident. While it clarifies the moments leading to the collapse, broader implications for the bridge's security and the ongoing conflict remain subject to continuous assessment.

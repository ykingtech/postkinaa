Football World Mourns Tenerife Striker Jesus de Miguel After On-Pitch Collision

By PostKina Editorial · 4 November 2026 at 13:02 Europe/Paris · 4 min read

The global football community is in mourning following the tragic death of Tenerife striker Jesus de Miguel. His passing, which occurred after an on-pitch collision, prompted widespread tributes during Tuesday's Champions League fixtures.

What happened

The football world is grappling with profound grief following the sudden death of Jesus de Miguel, a striker for Spanish club Tenerife. De Miguel reportedly passed away after sustaining injuries during an on-pitch collision. The tragic news prompted an immediate and widespread outpouring of tributes across Europe, most notably observed during Tuesday's Champions League fixtures, where moments of remembrance honoured the fallen player. The circumstances surrounding the collision and his subsequent death have cast a somber shadow over the sport, highlighting the inherent risks, however rare, that players face.

Background and context

Football, like many contact sports, carries inherent risks of injury, ranging from minor knocks to severe trauma. However, fatalities directly resulting from on-pitch collisions during professional matches are exceptionally uncommon in the modern era. Advances in sports medicine, refereeing protocols designed to protect players, and the use of protective equipment have significantly reduced the incidence of life-threatening injuries. When such a rare and tragic event does occur, it often prompts a period of introspection within the sport regarding player safety and welfare, even if the specific circumstances are unique. The collective response to De Miguel's death highlights the deep-seated empathy and solidarity that exists within the global football family, where players, coaches, and fans often view each other as part of a larger, interconnected community, united by their passion for the game.

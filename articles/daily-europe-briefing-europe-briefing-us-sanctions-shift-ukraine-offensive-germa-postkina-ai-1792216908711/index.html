Daily Europe Briefing: Europe Briefing: US Sanctions Shift, Ukraine Offensive, German Spy Trial, Papal Health

By PostKina Editorial · 17 October 2026 at 08:01 Europe/Paris · 4 min read

US President Trump waives Russia sanctions, impacting Ukraine's fight. Italy criticizes the move. A German spy scandal trial continues, and the Pope is pictured post-surgery.

How we report: This article adds context and explanation to attributed source reporting. AI may assist the editorial workflow; sources, corrections and licensing remain visible to readers.

What happened

United States President Donald Trump has waived several sanctions against Russia that were part of a new law designed to punish Moscow for its ongoing full-scale invasion of Ukraine. The White House stated that these specific sanctions were deemed contrary to United States national interests.

Key facts

  • President Trump waived certain Russia sanctions.
  • The waived sanctions were part of a new US law targeting Russia's actions in Ukraine.
  • The White House cited US national interests as the reason for the waivers.
  • An Italian minister criticized the US decision.
  • Ukraine's military is conducting an offensive in the Donbas region.
  • Starlink satellite internet is playing a role in the Ukrainian operation.
  • A high-security trial is underway in Hamburg, Germany, involving Christina Block.
  • Pope Francis has been pictured for the first time since undergoing surgery and a lung cancer diagnosis.

Why it matters

President Trump's decision to waive sanctions against Russia signals a potential shift in American policy towards Moscow, particularly concerning the conflict in Ukraine. This move could embolden Russia and undermine international efforts to isolate it economically. For Ukraine, whose military is engaged in a critical offensive, the waiver of sanctions, especially if it leads to reduced pressure on Russia, could have significant implications for its ability to regain territory and achieve a lasting peace. The US president's assertion of national interest over congressionally mandated sanctions raises questions about the future of US foreign policy and its commitment to allies.

European impact

The decision by the US to waive sanctions against Russia has drawn criticism from within Europe. Italy's Defence Minister, Guido Crosetto, stated that the US risks prolonging Russia's invasion of Ukraine rather than helping to end it. This divergence in approach highlights potential rifts within Western alliances regarding strategy towards Russia. The ongoing Ukrainian offensive in the Donbas, supported by technology like Starlink, is taking place against a backdrop of shifting international support, which could influence the battlefield dynamics and the broader geopolitical landscape of the continent. Separately, a high-profile spy scandal trial in Germany, concerning the alleged abduction of children, continues to capture public attention. In Rome, the health of Pope Francis, who recently underwent surgery and was diagnosed with lung cancer, is a matter of significant concern for Catholics worldwide and a subject of international interest.

Background and context

The United States has been a leading provider of military and financial aid to Ukraine since Russia's full-scale invasion began. A recent US law aimed to impose stringent sanctions on Russia, but President Trump's authority to waive certain provisions has been a point of contention. Ukraine's military operations, such as Operation Vivaldi in the Donbas, are crucial for its territorial integrity and sovereignty. The use of Starlink, a satellite internet service provided by SpaceX, has been vital for maintaining communication lines for Ukrainian forces. In Germany, the trial of Christina Block, accused of orchestrating the abduction of her children, has exposed deep family disputes and alleged links to espionage, drawing significant media scrutiny. Pope Francis's health has been a subject of public interest, with his recent diagnosis of lung cancer following surgery raising concerns about his well-being and the continuity of his papacy.

What happens next

It remains to be seen how President Trump's waiver of sanctions will affect diplomatic relations with European allies and the ongoing conflict in Ukraine. Further statements from the White House and European capitals are anticipated. The Ukrainian military's progress in the Donbas will be closely monitored, as will the impact of technological support like Starlink. The German trial of Christina Block is expected to continue, with further revelations potentially emerging. The health status of Pope Francis will be a key focus for the Vatican and the global Catholic community.

Krakow Shooting: Doctor Recounts Treating Suspect Amidst Market Square Chaos

By PostKina Editorial · 9 November 2026 at 13:01 Europe/Paris · 3 min read

A doctor describes treating the suspect of a shooting incident near Krakow's Main Market Square, highlighting the chaotic aftermath and the psychological toll on those involved.

How we report: This article adds context and explanation to attributed source reporting. AI may assist the editorial workflow; sources, corrections and licensing remain visible to readers.

What happened

Two doctors were dining near Krakow's Main Market Square when a gunman opened fire, causing panic and chaos. The incident led to multiple casualties and the subsequent apprehension of a suspect. One of the doctors present, who chose to remain unnamed, later treated the alleged perpetrator, describing the experience as treating "victims." The doctor's account offers a unique perspective on the immediate aftermath of the violence, focusing on the medical response and the psychological state of those involved.

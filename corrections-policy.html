CORRECTIONS POLICY

Help us keep the record accurate.

PostKina welcomes specific, evidence-based correction requests from readers, sources and rights holders.

How to report an error

Email lankaknot@gmail.com with the article URL, the statement you believe is incorrect, the proposed correction and a reliable supporting source. Readers may also use the “Suggest a correction” control on an article.

What happens next

  1. We review the published text and the evidence provided.
  2. We check the source material and seek clarification when necessary.
  3. If a material error is confirmed, we update the article.
  4. Significant corrections should be identified in the article; minor spelling or formatting fixes may be made without a separate notice.

Updates and removals

New developments may be added as updates. We do not normally remove accurate published material, but will review credible legal, privacy, safety and copyright requests individually.

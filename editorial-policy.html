EDITORIAL POLICY

Accuracy before speed.

PostKina aims to publish clear, original summaries and explainers grounded in identifiable sources. We do not knowingly fabricate facts, quotations, eyewitness claims or sources.

Attribution and sourcing

Articles identify the principal source used as background and link to it whenever practical. Claims should be attributed when they come from another publication, organisation or public statement. We distinguish verified information from analysis and uncertainty.

Original value

We do not aim to reproduce another publisher's article. Our stories should add useful structure through sections explaining what happened, why it matters, European impact, background, verified sourcing and likely next developments.

Independence and conflicts

Advertising, sponsorship or commercial relationships do not purchase favourable editorial treatment. Sponsored material must be labelled clearly. Material conflicts of interest should be disclosed.

Images and rights

We prioritise relevant images carrying a reusable licence, public-domain material or PostKina-owned artwork. Credits and licence information are displayed where available. Rights concerns may be sent to lankaknot@gmail.com.

Corrections

Substantiated errors are corrected promptly and transparently under our Corrections Policy.
